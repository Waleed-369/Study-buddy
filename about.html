About Me

Hi! I’m a BS Software Engineering student at PMAS Arid Agriculture University, Rawalpindi. I’m passionate about technology, programming, and building creative projects that can help students like me learn and grow together. This website Study Buddy is one of my ongoing projects, where I aim to collect and share study materials, notes, and past papers specifically for IT students of Arid University. As I continue my journey through university, I’ll keep improving this platform and adding more features to make it a valuable study resource for everyone.
